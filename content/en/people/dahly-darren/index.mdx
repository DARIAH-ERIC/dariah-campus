Dr Darren Dahly is the Principal Statistician of the HRB Clinical Research Facility University College Cork, and a Senior Lecturer in Research Methods in the UCC School of Public Health. He consults and collaborates on a wide variety of patient-focused studies in and around Cork, and teaches postgraduates about clinical trial study design and analysis, reproducible research methods, and critical appraisal skills.
