Roland Fleischhacker is the co-founder and CEO of Deepsearch GmbH, a Vienna-based software company specializing in neurosymbolic AI. He has been working in the IT industry for over 35 years. His work focuses on areas such as natural language understanding (NLU), business processes, process automation, and artificial neural networks. Recently, Roland was appointed to the University for Continuing Education Krems (UWK) to teach in the Generative AI Certificate Program.
