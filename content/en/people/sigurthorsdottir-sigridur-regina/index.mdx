Sigridur Regina Sigurthorsdottir is a PhD researcher at the University of Iceland and the National Gallery of Iceland, examining the use of artist interviews and artists' archives in decision-making regarding the preservation, conservation and exhibition of time-based media art. She has an MA in moving image archiving and preservation as well as an MA in film theory. She was previously collections manager at the Living Art Museum and is not a board member for the Living Art Museum as well as Skjaldborg Documentary Film Festival. She represents PhD students on the board of the Literature and Art Theory Institute.
