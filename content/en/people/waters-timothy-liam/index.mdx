Timothy Liam Waters is a Postdoctoral Researcher at University College Cork. As part of the ERC-funded NorseMap project, Liam leads the Politics & Identity strand, focusing on the use and appropriation of medieval Scandinavia's history, symbolism, and literature in contemporary governance and culture. In addition to his work on the reception of medieval Scandinavia, Liam also works extensively in New Materialist and Ecocritical approaches to Old Norse literature.
