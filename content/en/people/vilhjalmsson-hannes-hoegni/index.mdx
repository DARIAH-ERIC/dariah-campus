Hannes Högni Vilhjálmsson is a Professor of Computer Science at Reykjavík University, where he is doing research in the area of virtual humans and interactive virtual environments, with an emphasis on social interaction and experiences that have social impact. As the leader of the Socially Expressive Computing Group, he has led five large national project grants, supervised over 35 theses and taught courses on the theory and practice of virtual environments, as well as courses on game development and natural language technology. At Reykjavik University, he has also served as the chair of the Research Council and the director of the Centre for Analysis and Design of Intelligent Agents.
