Dr Brendan Palmer joined the HRB CRF-UCC in 2017. Brendan works within the Statistics, Data & Analysis Unit (SDAU) in a role that focuses on providing data management and statistical supports to academic-led clinical trials. This includes, but is not limited to, advice on the data needs of project pre-initiation, design and construction of electronic databases, data visualisation, analytical pipeline development, reproducible reporting and data publication supports.

Brendan also contributes to the MSc Clinical Trials ([CKU17](https://www.ucc.ie/en/ckx28/)) and delivery of the postgraduate module, Reproducible Research Practices using R ([PG6030](https://ucc-ie-public.courseleaf.com/modules/)). Brendan is a Health Research Board data steward and is in place to advise researchers on approaches to, and the implementation of, the [FAIR guiding principles for scientific data management and stewardship](https://www.nature.com/articles/sdata201618) for investigators engaged in patient focused research.
