Dr Darius Whelan is a Senior Lecturer in the School of Law, UCC.  His research and teaching interests include mental health law, Internet regulation and employment law.  He is Director of UCC’s LLM in Intellectual Property and E Law, co-founder of UCC’s IT law Clinic, and a member of Creative Commons.
