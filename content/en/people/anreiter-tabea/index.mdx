Tabea Anreiter studied Transcultural Communication at the University of Vienna and later taught there as a tutor, focusing on language technologies. Prior to that, she gained professional experience in design, marketing and communications. At the ACDH, she works as a Training Officer in the research unit DH Research & Infrastructure and contributes to the ARTEMIS project (Applying Reactive Twins to Enhance Monument Information Systems). &#x20;
