Paola Peratello is postdoctoral researcher at the École nationale des chartes – PSL (Paris) and previously she served as researcher at Ca’ Foscari University of Venice. She holds a PhD and the title of Doctor Europaeus in Germanic Philology from the University of Verona. Her doctoral research focused on the analysis and edition of the Codex Runicus (AM 28 8vo, Den Arnamagnæanske Samling, Copenhagen). Her research interests include runology, medieval Scandinavian manuscript studies, textual criticism, as well as the application of digital tools and methodologies to the analysis, preservation, and enhancement of cultural heritage.
