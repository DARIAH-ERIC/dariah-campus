Katrín Lísa L. Mikaelsdóttir holds a Ph.D. in Icelandic Linguistics from the University of Iceland. She currently works as an Adjunct professor in Medieval Icelandic Studies at the University of Iceland and as a project manager at the Centre for Digital Humanities and Arts in Reykjavík. She is also involved in several European training and research networks, including the Erasmus+ KA2-funded network ANTIDOTE – Advanced Training in Digitization of Older Texts, SCOOP – Source Codes of the Past: An international network for Automated Text Recognition, and is an organizer of the annual Winter School "HTR of Historical Sources". Her main research areas include historical linguistics, statistical analysis and data visualization, and advances in Automated Text Recognition.
