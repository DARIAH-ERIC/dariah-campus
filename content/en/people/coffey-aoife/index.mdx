Aoife Coffey is Research Data Lead at University College Cork (UCC) Library, where she supports the development of research data management, stewardship, and open research practices across the university. Her work focuses on building researcher capability in data management, advancing FAIR data principles and developing sustainable support services and training programmes for researchers. Aoife is a founding member of Sonraí Irish Data Stewardship Network and has been actively involved in national initiatives to strengthen data stewardship skills and careers including the Sonraí microcredential project and contributes to research and policy discussions on research data management, Open Research and research culture.
