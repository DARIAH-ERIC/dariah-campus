Prof. Richard Rogers is a Web epistemologist whose work focuses on how the Web functions as a distinct knowledge culture. His research explores opportunities made possible by the Internet and includes the development of digital research tools. One of his best-known tools is the Issue Crawler, which maps “issue networks” made up of organizations, documents, media objects and other actors connected around specific topics. Rogers is also central to the Digital Methods Initiative, which develops tools and methods for capturing and analysing Web data by repurposing online platforms and digital media practices for broader social and cultural research.
