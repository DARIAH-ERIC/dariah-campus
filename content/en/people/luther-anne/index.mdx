She holds a PhD from Central Saint Martins College of Art and Design, London, where she developed interactive data visualisation software for qualitative research. Her previous work includes research and coordination roles at the Parsons Institute for Information Mapping and the Center for Data Arts at Parsons School of Design in New York, with a focus on museum data, digital research methods and software development.

She has also held a Mellon Fellowship at the Price Lab for Digital Humanities at the University of Pennsylvania and has advised museums and cultural organisations on digitisation strategies, digital collection management and data-driven research.
