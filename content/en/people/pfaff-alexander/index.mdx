Alexander Pfaff is an Associate Professor at the Faculty of Languages and Cultures at the University of Iceland. With a background in Linguistics, Nordic Philology, and Icelandic, his research spans formal syntax and semantics, the syntax-morphology interface, and descriptive and historical linguistics. Having worked extensively on noun phrases in Icelandic and Germanic languages, he has increasingly shifted his attention towards computational and mathematical approaches to language in recent years.
