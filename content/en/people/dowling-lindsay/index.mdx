Lindsay Dowling is the Open Research Support Unit Lead at TU Dublin, specialising in data stewardship and the development of transparent, ethical, and FAIR-aligned research (data) practices. She supports researchers in managing, documenting, and sharing data responsibly across the research lifecycle, ensuring strong governance and long‑term value. Lindsay has a qualification in Data Stewardship from the University of Vienna and is an active member of Sonraí.
