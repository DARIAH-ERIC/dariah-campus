  content: >-
    This course introduces the fundamental principles of audiovisual grammar
    through six complementary modules: CUT, OBSERVE, HEAR, CONNECT, SHOW / HIDE
    and CHOOSE. Combining original video episodes, detailed analyses, key
    concepts and practical activities, it explores how editing, framing, sound,
    association, off-screen space and selection shape meaning and guide the
    viewer’s perception. Designed for teachers, trainers and anyone interested
    in image education, the course supports both critical analysis and
    audiovisual experimentation.
