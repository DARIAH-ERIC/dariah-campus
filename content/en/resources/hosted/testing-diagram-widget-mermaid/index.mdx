You can use mermaid to create a diagram from a code block.



```mermaid
flowchart TD;
Cookie Monster-->|wants| biscuits
biscuits-->|on Monday| chocolate chip;
biscuits-->|on Tuesday| custard cream;
```

Mermaid allows to visualise your information as diagrams.

<Diagram link={false}>
  <DiagramCodeBlock>
    ```mermaid
    graph LR;
      accTitle: Shakespeare is author of Hamlet.
      accDescr: A diagram showing that Shakespeare is the author of Hamlet.
      Shakespeare-->|is author of| Hamlet;
    ```
  </DiagramCodeBlock>

  <DiagramCaption>
    This diagram describes that Shakespeare is the author of the play Hamlet. The diagram describes the relationship of the entities Shakespeare and Hamlet.
  </DiagramCaption>
</Diagram>
