  content: >-
    Studying Religion with Digital Methods and Tools is an open learning
    resource that explores how digital humanities approaches can enrich the
    study of historical and contemporary religion. It introduces the basic
    principles of selected digital methods and uses case studies to show how
    they can be applied. It also discusses the kinds of research questions these
    methods make possible, as well as their opportunities and limitations. The
    resource emphasizes conceptual and methodological understanding rather than
    technical training.
