In this session, we’ll step back from day‑to‑day data work and look at the broader policy ecosystem that shapes how research is conducted. From European directives to national strategies, funder requirements, publisher expectations, and institutional policies, the landscape is complex—but it directly influences what researchers must do and how data stewards support them. Today’s lecture introduces this multilayered policy environment, explores how these layers intersect, and highlights why understanding them is essential for enabling responsible, ethical, high‑quality research. We also hear from Professor Pádraig Carmody, Trinity College Dublin on Knowledge Security and Data Sovereignty.

## Learning Outcomes&#x20;

1. Describe the key policies.
2. Recognise policy relevance in practice Identify stakeholder requirements.&#x20;
3. Understand the link between policy and research data management.&#x20;
4. Understand the need to balance open policies with legal and ethical responsibilities.
