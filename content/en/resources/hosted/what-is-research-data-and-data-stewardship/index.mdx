  content: >-
    This unit introduces research data stewardship and its importance for
    high-quality, ethical, and reproducible research. It explores the research
    data lifecycle, the roles of data stewards, and different stewardship
    models. Through practical examples, it highlights how effective stewardship
    supports good research practice, FAIR data, compliance, reuse, and open
    research, while reducing risks to research integrity. A key message is that
    data stewardship is a shared responsibility focused on enabling, not
    controlling, research.
