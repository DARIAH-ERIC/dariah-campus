## Introduction&#x20;

In this unit we look at Research Data Management and Data Management Plans (DMPs), highlighting why DMPs are now an essential in part of the research process and landscape. We will also  explore the policy landscape at national and international levels, demonstrate how funders embed FAIR principles into funding calls, and highlight the risks of poor data organisation for transparency, accountability, and reproducibility.

The session also explores what makes a strong DMP in practice, including documentation, metadata, costing, disciplinary considerations, and common challenges. Through practical tools, templates, checklists, and stewardship supports, the demonstrate that  data management is not an administrative burden, but as an essential component of research integrity, collaboration, and long-term research value.

## Learning Outcomes

1. Analyse the role of data steward and the research data lifecycle and the principles and practices of data management.
2. &#x20;Design and generate data management plans that comply with relevant policies, legislation, and codes of conduct.
3. Design, implement, and assess documentation and metadata standards to ensure effective data stewardship, accessibility, and compliance with best practices.
4. Apply and assess the FAIR and Open Data Principles across various contexts and data types.
5. &#x20;Evaluate and compare data repositories for use across data types and research needs.
