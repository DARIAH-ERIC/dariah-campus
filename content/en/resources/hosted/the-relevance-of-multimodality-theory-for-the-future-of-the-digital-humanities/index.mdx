  content: >-
    In this ACDH-CH Lecture, John Bateman explores how multimodal semiotics can
    contribute to the future of Digital Humanities. Moving beyond text-centred
    approaches, he introduces key concepts such as semiotic modes, materiality,
    media, discourse semantics, and corpus-based analysis. The lecture argues
    that multimodal theory offers principled methods for analysing complex
    cultural artefacts (such as film, comics, diagrams, and digital media) while
    supporting scalable, interdisciplinary research practices in the Digital
    Humanities.
