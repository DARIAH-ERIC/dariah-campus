  content: >-
    This session introduces the FAIR Principles, Findable, Accessible,
    Interoperable, and Reusable, and explores how they support research
    transparency, reproducibility, and long‑term data value. We’ll examine FAIR
    in practice through metadata, persistent identifiers, and repository
    selection, using discipline‑specific examples. The session highlights the
    partnership between researchers and data stewards, practical actions for
    Data Management Plans, and key tools and infrastructures. We will also
    consider the CARE Principles and their role in supporting ethical and
    responsible data governance.
