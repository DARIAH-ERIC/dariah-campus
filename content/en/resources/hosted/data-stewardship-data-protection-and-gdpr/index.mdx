This session will help you as data stewards connect GDPR requirements with everyday data stewardship. It covers managing personal data in research workflows, assessing compliant infrastructure, and clarifying roles and responsibilities across the research compliance landscape. The session also explores the changing compliance landscape, clarifying roles, responsibilities, and collaborations between data stewards, researchers, legal teams, and IT services.

## Learning Outcomes&#x20;

1. Navigate key regulations and connect legal requirements with data stewardship practice. ​
2. Provide RDM guidance on personal data handling in research workflows. ​
3. Help researchers identify and ensure infrastructure meets compliance requirements.
4. Distinguish roles and evolving responsibilities within the compliance ecosystem&#x20;
