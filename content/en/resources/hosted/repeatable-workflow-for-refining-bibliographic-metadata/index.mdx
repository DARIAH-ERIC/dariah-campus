  content: >-
    A repeatable workflow for turning bibliographic catalogue records into
    research-ready data. Using publication years and places in the English Short
    Title Catalogue (ESTC), it guides readers through understanding source
    records, planning shared data and processing conventions, refining,
    evaluating results, and publishing documented datasets. The examples show
    how to preserve original statements, uncertainty, and unresolved cases while
    making transformations and limitations traceable. The workflow is intended
    for researchers and data professionals, and can be adapted to other
    catalogues and research purposes.
