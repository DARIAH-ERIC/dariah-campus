This weeks lecture will introduce the essential principles of copyright and licensing as they relate to data stewardship, giving you a solid grounding in the basics of Irish and EU copyright law, how it differs internationally, and what this means for cross-border collaborations. You’ll also explore how to choose appropriate licenses for datasets, avoid common licensing  issues,  and  when to call in the experts and confidently guide researchers through these decisions as a data steward.

## Learning Outcomes

1. Understand the basics of EU and Irish copyright law.
2. Recognise how legal requirements differ between countries, especially for sharing data across borders.
3. Understand the main challenges and benefits when using or reusing existing (third-party) data in research.&#x20;
4. Select suitable licenses (like Creative Commons) and understand copyright risks when sharing or reusing data.

## Copyright and Creative Commons&#x20;

Dr Darius Whelan introduces the various types of Creative Commons (CC) licences and discuss their relevance for researchers in any discipline.   The legal tools provided by CC help those who want to encourage reuse of their works by offering them for use under generous, standardised terms; those who want to make creative uses of works; and those who want to benefit from this symbiosis.
