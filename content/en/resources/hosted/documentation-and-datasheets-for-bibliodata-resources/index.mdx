## Introduction

This training helps you to make your dataset of bibliographic data more accessible and reusable via creating a human- and machine-readable documentation.

## Learning outcomes

After the training you will be able to answer:&#x20;

1. What is a datasheet?&#x20;
2. Why is documentation important when publishing a collection of bibliographic data?
3. How to fill out the datasheet template alongside producing my bibliodata collection?
4. What are mandatory, recommended and desirable fields in the datasheet?
5. How to combine planing and managing your research project and documentation?
