  content: >-
    Bibliographic data in particular is not necessarily human readable text but
    rather consists of metadata mostly. Also, the composition of particicular
    collections of bibliodata are not clear and easily re-usable. The
    "datasheets for cultural heritage data-template" created by the Europeanapro
    group was intended for GLAM insititutions and diverse cultural heritage
    datasets and collections. This training adopts the idea for bibliodata and
    uses it for documentation, data management and increase in usability. It can
    also be used to document the workflow of how to create a bibliodata
    collection or dataset.

In order to make your dataset of bibliographic data more accessible and reusable, you should provide a human-readable documentation which could follow a given template like the Europeana Datasheet.
