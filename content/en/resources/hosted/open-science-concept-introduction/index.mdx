The video explains the concept of open science with some examples of open publications and open datasets. The benefits of open science are highlighted, and attention is paid to the costs of it.

This introductory video is part of a video library used as teaching resource for BA Course [Introduction to Helsinki Digital Humanities and Social Science](http://dhcr.clarin-dariah.eu/courses/view/907) at the University of Helsinki.

## **Learning Outcomes**

After watching this video, viewers will be able to:

* Have a picture of the various forms of open science, and understand why it is important, especially in the global perspective.
* Know why they should use open materials in their research and share the results of their research with open access.
