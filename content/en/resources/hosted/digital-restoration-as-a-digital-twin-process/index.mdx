  content: >-
    This learning resource introduces digital restoration and the role of
    Heritage Digital Twins in documenting, analysing and reconstructing
    cultural-heritage objects. It focuses on virtual anastylosis and
    computational reassembly, explaining key challenges such as fragment
    uncertainty, 3D data preparation, alignment and missing-geometry prediction.
    Emphasis is placed on the collaboration between computational methods and
    human expertise through user-in-the-loop approaches. The resource is aimed
    at learners in cultural heritage, archaeology, conservation, restoration and
    digital humanities, with no advanced technical background required.
