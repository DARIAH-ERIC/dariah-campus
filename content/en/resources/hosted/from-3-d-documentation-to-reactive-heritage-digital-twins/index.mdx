  content: >-
    Cultural heritage faces growing risks from climate change, disasters,
    degradation, and conflict, making reliable documentation essential for
    preservation. This learning resource introduces 3D documentation as a
    methodological process for recording, analysing, managing, and communicating
    heritage knowledge. It explains key survey methods, modelling approaches,
    metadata, paradata, interoperability, and FAIR data principles, emphasizing
    reliability and reuse over visual appearance alone. It also addresses
    emerging developments, including AI-supported workflows, different 3D model
    types, and Reactive Heritage Digital Twins for conservation, research, and
    public engagement.
