In this unit we  introduce the key concepts of data curation, archiving, and preservation within the research data lifecycle. It explores how data stewards and researchers can make informed decisions about managing, selecting, and safeguarding research data to ensure long-term usability, reproducibility, and compliance with policy and ethical requirements. We also consider in short how we make informed decisions about active data storage during the lifetime of the project.&#x20;

We also hear from Lindsay Dowling, Open Research Support Unit Lead at Technological University Dublin about her experiences working with researchers and on research projects as a data steward.&#x20;

## Learning Outcomes&#x20;

* Distinguish between curation, archiving, and preservation and identify which approach to use in different contexts.
* Use selection criteria to appraise a dataset and justify retention decisions&#x20;
* Identify risks to long-term persistence of the data, and recommend actions to safely preserve them.
