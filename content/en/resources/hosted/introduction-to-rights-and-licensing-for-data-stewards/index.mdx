In this unit will introduce the essential principles of copyright and licensing as they relate to data stewardship, giving you a solid grounding in the basics of Irish and EU copyright law, how it differs internationally, and what this means for cross-border collaborations. You’ll also explore how to choose appropriate licenses for datasets, avoid common licensing  issues,  and  when to call in the experts and confidently guide researchers through these decisions as a data steward.

We also hear from Dr. Darius Whelan, College of Business and Law, University College Cork about copyright and the Creative Commons licenses.&#x20;

## Learning Outcomes&#x20;

* Understand the basics of EU and Irish copyright law.
* Recognise how legal requirements differ between countries, especially for sharing data across borders.
* Understand the main challenges and benefits when using or reusing existing (third-party) data in research
* Select suitable licenses (like Creative Commons) and understand copyright risks when sharing or reusing data.
