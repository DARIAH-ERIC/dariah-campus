  content: >-
    This resource introduces semantic modelling for Heritage Digital Twins,
    focusing on how ontologies and knowledge graphs enable interoperability
    across heterogeneous cultural heritage data. Using the ARTEMIS framework, it
    explains how physical heritage, digital representations, scientific
    observations, workflows and services can be connected through shared
    concepts, explicit relationships and provenance. Learners are introduced to
    the ARTEMIS ontology architecture and its key principles for building
    interoperable, traceable and extensible Reactive Heritage Digital Twins.
