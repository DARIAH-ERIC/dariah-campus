  title: AI Winter School 2026
  content: >-
    The DARIAH-Campus resource on the AI Winter School 2026 contains three video
    recordings covering key topics in artificial intelligence, generative
    models, and machine learning. The videos provide insights into current
    developments, methodological approaches, and practical questions related to
    the use of AI technologies in research and digital humanities contexts.


    The first video, “Vibe Coding & Promptotyping” by Christopher Pollin,
    introduces current approaches to AI-assisted software development. It
    focuses on concepts such as Vibe Coding, Context Engineering, and the
    Promptotyping methodology. These approaches demonstrate how researchers
    without extensive programming skills can use Large Language Models to
    develop their own data-driven tools, interfaces, models, and workflows.


    The second video, “Open EuroLLM” by Patrick Rarivoson, addresses current
    developments in the field of European Large Language Models. The talk
    explores the significance of open language models, their technical and
    societal contexts, and their role in research, innovation, and digital
    sovereignty in Europe.


    The third video, “Bias in ML” by Philip Winter, offers an accessible
    introduction to bias in machine learning. After a brief overview of
    fundamental machine learning concepts, the talk examines different forms of
    social and technical bias in modern deep learning applications. It also
    discusses the potential consequences of such biases and presents practical
    strategies for identifying and mitigating them.


    Together, the three videos document central themes of the AI Winter School
    2026 and provide a concise resource for anyone interested in AI-assisted
    research, open language models, and the responsible use of machine learning.
